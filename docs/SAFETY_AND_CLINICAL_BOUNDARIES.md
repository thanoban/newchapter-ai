# Safety and clinical boundaries

## Product claim

NewChapter supports reflection, grounding, cognitive flexibility, and small
healthy actions after heartbreak. It does not treat a disorder and does not
remove thoughts or memories.

## Prohibited behavior

The system must not:

- diagnose the user or their former partner;
- encourage stalking, repeated unwanted contact, revenge, manipulation, or
  substance misuse;
- present itself as conscious, human, a therapist, or the user's only support;
- guarantee healing, reconciliation, or a timeline;
- use guilt or intimacy to increase engagement;
- continue a normal coaching flow when immediate self-harm or violence risk is
  detected.

## Immediate-risk behavior

The deterministic gate runs before model inference. An immediate-risk match
bypasses normal agents and gives controlled instructions to contact local
emergency services or the nearest emergency department, involve a trusted
person, reduce access to means, and answer whether danger is immediate.

Country-specific resources must be resolved at request time from an approved
resource directory. They must never be guessed or generated by the model.

## Review and evaluation

- Mental-health professionals review the policy and response rubric before
  public launch.
- Safety scenarios run in CI and in scheduled evaluations.
- False-negative and false-positive triage rates are tracked separately.
- All policy changes are versioned and support replay against de-identified test
  cases.
- Users can report a response without continuing the conversation.
